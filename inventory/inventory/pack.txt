- DropTable (core/databases/DropTable.gd): Manages weighted random selection for item drops.
- EcologyManager (core/systems/ecology/EcologyManager.gd): Manages all regions, drives daily ecology updates, and issues spawn requests.
- EcologyObservationUI (scenes/UI/EcologyObservationUI.gd): 
- Entity (scenes/Core/Entity.gd): Base class for all world entities providing fundamental properties and lifecycle signals.

- GameState (core/autoload/GameState.gd): Acts as the central global authority for managing game progression, economy, status effects, environment, and data loading/unloading.
